# Articles of Faith — the ten stated doctrines of the True Jesus Church (TJC).
# Use these only when the text is *teaching or defending the doctrine itself*.
# A personal story about receiving a doctrine belongs under /Testimonies.
# ===========================================================================
/Articles of Faith :: Direct exposition or defence of one of the ten Articles of Faith of the True Jesus Church as formal doctrine. Use the parent only when several articles are surveyed together (e.g. a "Ten Articles of Faith" overview or new-believer doctrinal series); otherwise pick the specific article.
/Articles of Faith/Jesus Christ, the One True God :: The doctrine that Jesus Christ is the one true God — the Word made flesh, crucified, resurrected and ascended, the only Saviour, and that God is one rather than three persons. Includes TJC teaching against Trinitarianism, the deity and humanity of Christ, and baptism "in the name of Jesus Christ" as it bears on God's oneness.
/Articles of Faith/Holy Bible :: The doctrine that the Old and New Testaments are inspired by God, the sole standard of faith and the only rule for belief and conduct. Use for texts asserting the Bible's authority, sufficiency and completeness as an article of faith.
/Articles of Faith/One True Church, True Jesus Church :: The doctrine that the True Jesus Church, established by the outpouring of the Holy Spirit in the latter rain, is the restored apostolic church and the one true church of salvation. Includes the marks of the true church and TJC's identity claim.
/Articles of Faith/Water Baptism :: The doctrine of water baptism for the remission of sins — administered in natural living water, in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, with the head bowed and face downward, by a baptised worker. Use for doctrinal exposition of the mode, formula, efficacy and necessity of baptism.
/Articles of Faith/Holy Spirit :: The doctrine that receiving the Holy Spirit, evidenced by speaking in tongues, is the guarantee of the heavenly inheritance. Use for teaching on the promise, evidence, purpose and pledge of the Spirit as an article of faith.
/Articles of Faith/Footwashing :: The doctrine of the footwashing sacrament — that it grants a part with the Lord, and teaches love, holiness, humility, forgiveness and service. Use for doctrinal exposition of its institution, mode and meaning.
/Articles of Faith/Holy Communion :: The doctrine of the Holy Communion sacrament — one unleavened bread and grape juice, the flesh and blood of the Lord, for eternal life and resurrection at the last day, expressing the one body of believers.
/Articles of Faith/Sabbath Day :: The doctrine that the seventh day, Saturday, is the holy Sabbath to be kept under grace in commemoration of God's creation and salvation, with the hope of eternal rest. Use for doctrinal argument for the Sabbath; put practical how-to keeping under /Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Sabbath Observance.
/Articles of Faith/Salvation by Grace through Faith :: The doctrine that salvation is by God's grace through faith, that believers must rely on the Holy Spirit to pursue holiness, honour God and love others, and must endure to the end. Use for the grace-faith-works relationship as formal doctrine.
/Articles of Faith/Second Coming :: The doctrine that the Lord will descend from heaven on the last day to judge the world, the righteous receiving eternal life and the wicked eternal punishment. Use for the doctrinal statement; put detailed end-times study under /Eschatology and Last Things.

# Bible and Truth — Scripture, doctrine, biblical people, places and books.
# ===========================================================================
/Bible and Truth :: Broad biblical or doctrinal teaching that does not settle into any single child below. Prefer a specific child whenever the text has an identifiable subject.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics :: Thematic biblical study of a practice or subject that runs across Scripture. Use the parent only when several such topics are surveyed together.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Prayer and Fasting :: Teaching on prayer and fasting — kinds of prayer, posture and attitude, praying in the Spirit and in tongues, persistence, intercession, unanswered prayer, and the discipline of fasting.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Psalms and Hymns and Spiritual Songs :: Teaching on sacred music and hymnody — the place of hymns in worship, singing in the Spirit, hymn exposition and commentary, choirs and music ministry. Not for warnings about worldly music, which belong under /Daily Living/Personal Life/Secular Music.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Miracles and Divine Healing :: Teaching on miracles and divine healing as a biblical subject — why and how God heals, the relationship of faith to healing, prayer for the sick, anointing, and the purpose of signs. Personal accounts of being healed belong under /Testimonies/Healing and Health.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Angels :: Teaching on angels — their nature, ranks, ministry to believers, appearances in Scripture, and the limits of angelic worship.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Satan, Demons and Spiritual Warfare :: Teaching on Satan, evil spirits, possession and deliverance, temptation as spiritual attack, the armour of God and resisting the devil.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Tithes and Offerings :: Teaching on tithing, freewill offerings, vows and the biblical principles of giving to God. Broader personal money management belongs under /Daily Living/Social Life/Money and Stewardship.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Sabbath Observance :: Practical teaching on how to keep the Sabbath — preparation, rest from work and commerce, worship attendance, permissible activity, travel, school and employment conflicts. Doctrinal argument for the Sabbath belongs under /Articles of Faith/Sabbath Day.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Eternal Hope :: Teaching on the believer's hope of eternal life and the heavenly kingdom as motivation for present endurance — pilgrimage, treasures in heaven, the eternal weight of glory.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Worship and Idolatry :: Teaching on true worship of God versus idolatry — worship in spirit and truth, images and idols in Scripture, reverence in the house of God, syncretism. Contemporary idol and ancestor practices belong under /Pagan Traditions and Worldly Practices.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/The Law and the Gospel :: Teaching on the relationship between the Mosaic law and the gospel — ceremonial versus moral law, the law as tutor, the commandments under grace, legalism and licence.
/Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Numbers and Symbols in Scripture :: Teaching on biblical numerology, typology and symbolism — sevens, forties, the tabernacle furnishings, feasts as types, and figurative language in Scripture.
/Bible and Truth/Scripture :: Study of the Bible as a book — its formation, inspiration and interpretation. Use the parent for general "how we got the Bible" or "how to read the Bible" pieces that span the children.
/Bible and Truth/Scripture/Canon :: The formation and boundaries of the biblical canon — which books belong, apocrypha and pseudepigrapha, manuscripts, transmission and translations.
/Bible and Truth/Scripture/Inspiration :: The inspiration, inerrancy, authority and preservation of Scripture — how God spoke through human authors, and the trustworthiness of the text.
/Bible and Truth/Scripture/Interpretation and Hermeneutics :: Principles and methods of interpreting Scripture — context, genre, comparing Scripture with Scripture, typology, guarding against private interpretation, and Bible study method.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies :: Study of a biblical person's life, character and lessons drawn from it. Use the parent only for pieces treating many unrelated figures at once.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament :: An Old Testament figure or group of figures not covered by a more specific child below.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Primeval History :: Figures and events of Genesis 1-11 treated as narrative history — creation, the fall, Cain and Abel, the flood generation, Babel and the early genealogies.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Patriarchs :: The patriarchal fathers as a group, or a patriarch without his own child node.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Patriarchs/Adam :: Adam — his creation, the image of God, the fall, and Adam as a type of Christ.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Patriarchs/Noah :: Noah — his righteousness in a corrupt generation, the ark, the flood, the covenant of the rainbow, and Noah as a type of salvation.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Patriarchs/Abraham :: Abraham — the call from Ur, the covenant and promise, justification by faith, the offering of Isaac, and Abraham as the father of the faithful.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Patriarchs/Isaac :: Isaac — the child of promise, his submission on Mount Moriah, his marriage, wells and blessings, and his quiet faith.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Patriarchs/Jacob :: Jacob — the birthright and deception, Bethel, Laban, wrestling at Peniel, his renaming to Israel, and his shaping by God over a lifetime.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Patriarchs/Esau :: Esau — despising the birthright, the lost blessing, and Esau as a warning about the flesh and profane living.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Patriarchs/Job :: Job — his integrity, the loss of family and health, his friends' arguments, God's answer out of the whirlwind, and suffering with faith.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Women :: An Old Testament woman as a group study, or one without her own child node (e.g. Rahab, Abigail, Naomi, Miriam, Jezebel treated as a woman's study).
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Women/Eve :: Eve — the first woman, the helper, the temptation and fall, and the promise of the seed.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Women/Sarah :: Sarah — the long wait for a son, laughter and doubt, Hagar and Ishmael, and Sarah as an example of a submissive and faithful wife.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Women/Rebeccah :: Rebecca — the drawing of water at the well, her willingness to go, the twin sons, and her part in Jacob's deception.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Women/Ruth :: Ruth — leaving Moab, loyalty to Naomi, gleaning in Boaz's field, and redemption through the kinsman-redeemer.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Women/Hannah :: Hannah — barrenness and reproach, her vow and prayer at Shiloh, and the dedication of Samuel.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Women/Esther :: Esther — the Persian court, Mordecai's charge, "if I perish, I perish", the deliverance of the Jews and God's hidden providence.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers :: An Old Testament prophet, judge or servant of God as a group study, or one without a child node below.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Joseph :: Joseph — the dreams, betrayal by his brothers, Potiphar's house, prison, exaltation in Egypt, forgiveness of his brothers, and Joseph as a type of Christ.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Moses :: Moses — the basket and the palace, the burning bush, the exodus and plagues, the law at Sinai, intercession for Israel, meekness, and the failure at Meribah.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Aaron :: Aaron — the first high priest, spokesman for Moses, the golden calf, the priestly garments and censer, and priesthood as a type.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Joshua :: Joshua — the faithful spy, succeeding Moses, the crossing of Jordan, Jericho, the conquest and division of the land, and "choose you this day".
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Deborah :: Deborah — the judge and prophetess under the palm tree, the call of Barak, the victory over Sisera, and her song.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Samson :: Samson — the Nazirite vow, his strength and its source, Delilah, blindness at the mill, and his final prayer.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Samuel :: Samuel — "speak, for thy servant heareth", the last judge, anointing Saul and David, and his intercession and farewell.

/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Nathan :: Nathan — the prophet sent to David, "thou art the man", the promise of an everlasting house, and courageous rebuke.
/Bible and Truth/Character Studies/Old Testament/Prophets, Servants, Messengers/Elijah :: Elijah — the drought and the ravens, the widow of Zarephath, Carmel and the fire from heaven, the juniper tree and the still small voice, and the chariot of fire.

/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Faithfulness :: Faithfulness and reliability before God and people — keeping one's word and vows, steadfastness in duty, being a good and faithful servant, and faithfulness in small things.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Holiness :: Holiness and sanctified living in general — being set apart, "be ye holy for I am holy", separation from the world, and the pursuit of a holy walk. Use a child below when the emphasis is specifically purity, goodness or godliness.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Holiness/Purity :: Purity of heart, mind and body — clean thoughts, modesty in dress and conduct, guarding the eyes, and keeping oneself unspotted. Sexual purity as a distinct struggle belongs under /Daily Living/Personal Life/Sexual Purity.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Holiness/Goodness :: Goodness, integrity and uprightness — doing good works, honesty and fair dealing, kindness as settled character, and a good conscience before God and man.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Holiness/Godliness :: Godliness and reverence — the fear of the Lord, piety in daily life, godliness with contentment, and a life ordered around God rather than self.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Knowledge :: Spiritual knowledge and understanding — growing in the knowledge of God and his word, knowledge with love, and the difference between head knowledge and spiritual understanding.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Knowledge/Wisdom and Discernment :: Wisdom and discernment — the fear of the Lord as its beginning, wise versus foolish choices, discerning truth from error and spirit from flesh, prudence and good judgment.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Knowledge/Knowing God’s will :: Seeking and recognising God's will for one's life — guidance in decisions about study, work, marriage and ministry; waiting on God; testing impressions; and submitting plans to him.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Obedience and Submission :: Obedience to God and submission to authority — hearing and doing, partial versus full obedience, submission to church leaders, elders and one another, and obedience when it is costly.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Humility :: Humility and lowliness of mind — esteeming others better than oneself, the mind of Christ, guarding against pride and self-exaltation, accepting correction, and serving without recognition.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Perseverance and Patience :: Endurance and long-suffering — running the race, holding fast to the end, patience in waiting on God and in bearing with people, and not growing weary in doing good.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Love :: Christian love in practice — the greatest commandment, love as described in 1 Corinthians 13, loving the brethren and one's enemies, forbearance and forgiveness in love, and love as the proof of discipleship.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Mercy :: Mercy and compassion — showing kindness to the poor, the sick, the stranger and the sinner; visiting and relieving those in need; and being merciful as the Father is merciful.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Service :: Serving God and others — willingness to be used, serving in unseen tasks, motives in service, avoiding burnout and grumbling, and the servant as the greatest in the kingdom. Serving in a formal church role belongs under /Ministry.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Sacrifice :: Self-denial and sacrifice — taking up the cross, offering the body a living sacrifice, giving up career, wealth, comfort or ambition for the Lord, and counting the cost.
/Daily Living/Christian Virtues/Bearing Witness :: Living as a witness for Christ — letting one's light shine, a consistent testimony before family, colleagues and classmates, and answering for one's faith in ordinary conversation. Organised evangelistic work belongs under /Ministry/Evangelism and Outreach.
/Daily Living/Church Life :: Life together in the local church — attending services and prayer meetings, punctuality and reverence, fellowship and hospitality, participation in church duties, relationships and conflict among brethren, unity, and the believer's responsibility to the congregation.
/Daily Living/Children and youth in church :: Children, students and young people in the life of the church — religious education classes, youth and student fellowship, spiritual convocations and camps, keeping young people in the faith, and the transition from youth to adult membership. Parenting in the home belongs under /Daily Living/Family Life/Parenting and Children.
/Daily Living/Family Life :: The Christian household where no child below fits — the family as a unit, family worship and altar, and the household of faith.
/Daily Living/Family Life/Honoring Parents :: Honouring father and mother — obedience in childhood, respect and care of ageing parents, filial duty in Chinese family culture, and honouring parents who are unbelieving or difficult.
/Daily Living/Family Life/Love, Courtship and Marriage :: Dating, courtship and choosing a spouse — seeking God's will in marriage, guarding purity during courtship, marrying in the Lord, interfaith relationships and unequal yoking, and preparation for marriage.
/Daily Living/Family Life/Wedding and Marriage Roles :: The wedding itself and the ordering of married life — the marriage covenant, the roles of husband and wife, headship and submission, mutual love and respect, communication and finances in marriage, and holy matrimony as a picture of Christ and the church.
/Daily Living/Family Life/Marriage Difficulties, Divorce and Remarriage :: Troubled marriages — conflict, unfaithfulness and adultery, separation, the biblical grounds for divorce, remarriage, single parenting after a broken marriage, and the church's counsel to struggling couples.
/Daily Living/Family Life/Parenting and Children :: Raising children in the faith — training and discipline, family devotions, teaching children to pray, education choices, screen and media rules, adolescence and rebellion, and praying for wayward children.
/Daily Living/Family Life/Loss and Grief in the Family :: Death and mourning in the family — bereavement of a spouse, parent, child or infant; miscarriage and stillbirth; funerals and Christian mourning; comforting the grieving; and hope for the departed in the Lord.
/Daily Living/Family Life/In-Laws, Extended Family and Family Rejection :: The wider family — relations with in-laws, multi-generational households, unbelieving relatives, opposition or persecution from family because of faith, and being disowned or pressured over baptism and Sabbath keeping.
/Daily Living/Personal Life :: The believer's inner and private life where no child below fits.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Devotionals :: Short devotional and meditative pieces built on a verse or image, written for daily reading and personal reflection rather than as structured teaching.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Speech :: The believer's own speech and words — truthfulness, edifying speech, avoiding profanity, complaining, boasting and idle words, and the discipline of silence. Gossip and slander in social settings belong under /Daily Living/Social Life/Speech and the Tongue.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Identity in Christ :: Who the believer is in Christ — a child of God, a new creation, chosen and beloved; self-worth and self-image grounded in God rather than achievement, appearance or the opinions of others.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Spiritual Growth and Maturity :: Growing from spiritual infancy to maturity — milk and meat, the marks of growth and of stagnation, backsliding and renewal, lukewarmness, and taking responsibility for one's own spiritual progress.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Overcoming Sin and Temptation :: The fight against sin — the nature of temptation, fleeing and resisting, the flesh against the spirit, besetting sins, self-examination, and victory through the Spirit.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Trials and Tribulations :: Suffering and hardship in the believer's life — the purpose of trials, refining through fire, persecution and loss, doubt and complaint under affliction, and holding faith when God seems silent.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Health, Body and Stewardship of the Body :: The body as the temple of the Holy Spirit — rest, diet, exercise, medical treatment and the balance of prayer with medicine, chronic illness and disability, and honouring God with one's physical life.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Mental and Emotional Wellbeing :: The mind and emotions — anxiety and worry, depression, loneliness, anger, fear, stress and burnout, grief, suicidal thoughts, counselling and therapy, and casting one's care upon God.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Forgiveness and Repentance :: Repentance and forgiveness in personal life — godly sorrow and confession, forsaking sin, receiving God's forgiveness, forgiving those who wronged us, reconciliation, and refusing to hold a grudge.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Habits, Addictions and Vices :: Habits and bondages — smoking, alcohol and drunkenness, drugs, gambling, gaming and internet addiction, overeating, and the process of breaking a habit by God's help.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Sexual Purity :: Sexual holiness — fornication and adultery of the heart, pornography and masturbation, homosexuality and gender questions treated pastorally, cohabitation, guarding the eyes and thoughts, and restoration after sexual sin.
/Daily Living/Personal Life/Secular Music :: The Christian's relationship to worldly entertainment and music — popular music, films, television, novels, video games and social media; discernment about content; and choosing hymns and edifying material instead.
/Daily Living/Social Life :: The believer among people outside the church where no child below fits.
/Daily Living/Social Life/In the Community :: Living among neighbours and society — friendships with unbelievers, community involvement and volunteering, being a good neighbour and citizen, and social pressures on the believer's conduct.
/Daily Living/Social Life/In the Workplace :: Faith at work — diligence and honesty on the job, difficult bosses and colleagues, workplace ethics and compromise, Sabbath and job conflicts, ambition and career choices, unemployment, and witnessing to co-workers.
/Daily Living/Social Life/On Campus :: Faith at school and university — study and examinations, academic pressure, campus friendships and peer pressure, student fellowship, leaving home for college, and keeping faith in a secular academic environment.
/Daily Living/Social Life/Money and Stewardship :: Personal finances — earning, saving and spending, debt, contentment versus materialism and greed, the love of money, wealth and poverty, insurance and investment, and generosity to those in need. Tithes and church offerings belong under /Bible and Truth/Biblical Topics/Tithes and Offerings.
/Daily Living/Social Life/Speech and the Tongue :: Speech among others — gossip, slander, backbiting and rumour, quarrelling and harsh words, flattery, keeping confidences, and speech on social media and in group chats.
/Daily Living/Social Life/Christian Witness in Society :: The believer's public stand — living distinctly in a secular culture, moral courage on social issues, reputation and integrity before outsiders, and the church's credibility through the conduct of its members.

/Testimonies/Family and Relationship :: Accounts centred on family and relationships — a spouse or parent brought to faith, reconciliation in a marriage or with a parent or child, a wayward child returning, grief over a family member, infertility and adoption, and God's work in courtship and marriage.
/Testimonies/Other :: A personal testimony whose subject does not fit any other testimony node — for example a general thanksgiving account, a mixed account spanning many subjects, or a brief note of praise.
